# OYP-1606 About settings audit

The About policy exposes bounded primary rows for version/build identity, open-source licenses, privacy, and legal/source-service information. Version/build values are supplied from the app build configuration rather than hard-coded release identity.

Diagnostics/export remains optional as permitted by the TODO; the policy records that explicitly rather than inventing an export implementation. Privacy copy states that local media/playback state remains on-device unless diagnostics are explicitly shared, and the legal notice identifies source services as independent third parties whose terms and applicable law still govern use.
